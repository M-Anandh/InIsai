# Privacy Policy for InIsai (இனிசை)

**Effective Date:** November 2026

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Account Information:** When you register, we collect your email address and profile name via Supabase Auth.
- **Listening Activity & Playlists:** Your created playlists, liked songs, and listening history are stored in our secure database if you are logged in.
- **Local Audio Files:** When you select audio files from your device to play via InIsai's Local Music feature, the audio files remain exclusively on your device. They are **never** transmitted to or stored on our servers.

## 2. How We Use Information
We use your information solely to:
- Provide personalized music streaming, playlist management, and playback features.
- Maintain your preferences across devices when authenticated.

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion
You can delete your playlists, history, or your entire account directly through the Settings page at any time.
